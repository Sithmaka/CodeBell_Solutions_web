### Why CodeBell section

**H2:** A delivery partner focused on clarity, reliability, and long-term use.

- **Start with the business problem:** Discovery defines users, workflows, risks, and a useful first release.
- **Make delivery visible:** Agree milestones, decision points, responsibilities, and communication before work begins.
- **Build for real operations:** Address access, data, integrations, testing, handover, and support—not only the interface.
- **Keep important decisions human:** AI can assist analysis and drafting; pricing, scope, contracts, and release decisions remain with accountable people.

Only retain these points if CodeBell can operationally evidence them.

### Process section

**H2:** From first conversation to a supported release.

1. **Understand:** Clarify the business problem, users, constraints, and success criteria.
2. **Define:** Agree scope, solution approach, responsibilities, risks, and delivery plan.
3. **Design and build:** Work in reviewable stages with regular feedback.
4. **Test and prepare:** Validate functionality, usability, security needs, data, and release readiness.
5. **Launch and improve:** Handover, monitor, support, and prioritize the next improvements.

## FAQ rewrite proposals

Use accessible disclosure buttons and answer only from verified operating policy.

1. **What does CodeBell build?**  
   CodeBell plans, designs, develops, integrates, and supports custom software, business systems, web and mobile applications, e-commerce experiences, and workflow automation. The exact approach depends on the business problem and existing systems.

2. **Do I need a complete specification before contacting you?**  
   No. A useful first enquiry explains the current problem, affected users, existing tools, desired outcome, and any important deadline or constraint. Discovery can turn that context into a clearer scope.

3. **How is a project scoped and priced?**  
   Scope and price depend on users, workflows, features, integrations, data, security needs, content, and delivery constraints. CodeBell should confirm these through discovery before making a proposal. Add deposit/currency details only from approved policy.

4. **How long does development take?**  
   Timing depends on scope, decision speed, integrations, data/content readiness, testing, and release requirements. CodeBell should provide an estimate after discovery rather than promise a generic timeline.

5. **Who owns the finished work?**  
   Ownership and licence terms should be stated in the project agreement. Current general terms say final rights depend on the agreed deliverables and full payment (`src/pages/Terms.jsx:201-217`).

6. **Can CodeBell integrate with our existing systems?**  
   Integration feasibility depends on available APIs, data access, vendor restrictions, security, and data quality. A technical review should confirm the right approach.

7. **What support is available after launch?**  
   Publish only verified warranty, maintenance, monitoring, and response options. Distinguish project defect correction from enhancements and ongoing support.

8. **How does CodeBell use AI?**  
   CodeBell may use AI-assisted tools for first-line support and internal drafting. Pricing, contracts, release decisions, and other commitments remain subject to human confirmation, consistent with `src/pages/Support.jsx:340-346` and `src/components/chatbot/Chatbot.jsx:949-952`.

9. **Can CodeBell work with clients outside Sri Lanka?**  
   Replace with verified details about countries served, remote meeting hours, contract currency, payment methods, and support coverage. Do not imply international delivery until operationally confirmed.

## Trust-building content suggestions

Publish only after verification:

- Named leadership and delivery roles with real biographies.
- Company legal/trading identity, registration and address where appropriate.
- Permissioned client logos with accessible names.
- Case studies with client-approved outcome evidence and time period.
- Security/QA practice: code review, testing, access control, backup, release, incident handling.
- Delivery artifacts: discovery summary, roadmap, change log, test/release checklist, handover package.
- Support/maintenance boundaries and response model.
- Technology partner certifications only if active and verifiable.
- Testimonials attributed to an authorized named person and organization with permission.

Never manufacture testimonials, metrics, awards, certifications, partner badges, or international offices.

## Editorial checklist

Before publishing any rewrite:

- Can delivery/sales prove every factual statement?
- Does the page identify a real audience, problem, and outcome?
- Does it explain how CodeBell reduces buyer risk?
- Is the CTA specific and consistent with the destination?
- Is there permission for every client/logo/result?
- Are service names consistent with forms, chatbot, CRM, and analytics?
- Are privacy, AI, currency, timezone, and cross-brand statements accurate?
- Does the page remain useful without a marketing claim or invented metric?
